# Medism — Creative Direction

**Brand idea:** *Expert healthcare guidance, from first conversation to recovery.*
**Supporting thought:** *Your care journey, coordinated.*

## The concept: the Care Route

Medism's job is to turn an overwhelming, unfamiliar journey into one clear route.
The design makes that literal. A single fine sea-green line with named checkpoints
— the **Care Route** — is the site's signature device. It appears as the drawn
rail beside the six-step journey, as quiet dotted paths inside image placeholders,
and ends at a clay-colored node: the human moment of recovery.

This evolves the existing MedIsm "Care Passport" identity
(`design-system/medism/MASTER.md` in the RLTRS repo) rather than replacing it:
same route-and-checkpoint thesis, same botanical palette family, same
no-fabrication discipline.

## Voice

Calm, precise, honest. Sentences short and translation-friendly. The site never
sells; it explains. Claims are either verifiable service descriptions or visibly
labeled placeholders. The recurring emotional register is *"someone responsible,
nearby"* — never urgency, never miracle language.

## Palette

Warm porcelain field; deep pine ink; pine for trust and action; sea for the
route; celadon for quiet panels; **clay reserved for human moments** (the
italic phrase in a headline, the recovery node, disclosure notes). Clay is never
used for decoration or generic CTAs.

## Typography

- **Newsreader** (editorial serif, optical sizing, real italics) — large
  statements only: hero, section headlines, the patient-story quote. Its italic
  in clay is the brand's single expressive gesture.
- **Figtree** (warm geometric-humanist sans) — everything else: UI, body,
  navigation, forms. Carried over from the existing MedIsm system for
  continuity.

One deliberate deviation from the old MASTER.md: that document banned editorial
serifs; the new brief explicitly asks for one. The serif is confined to large
statements so body reading stays entirely in the legible sans.

## Photography direction

Documentary, unposed, warm natural light. Coordinators guiding, specialists in
real conversation with patients, recovery at home. Eye contact and hands; no
staged handshakes, no crossed-arm doctors, no procedures, no tourism clichés.
Until Medism supplies rights-confirmed photography, every image slot renders an
art-directed placeholder that states the required shot — the photo brief ships
inside the design (see `components/Photo.tsx`).

## The aesthetic risk

A healthcare site that shows its placeholders. Unverified statistics, partner
names, and testimonials are not faked — the design treats "published only after
verification" as a visible trust feature (coral disclosure notes, labeled
profile templates, a reserved patient-story stage). The honesty is the luxury.
